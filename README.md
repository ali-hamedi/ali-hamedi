### About

I'm a Computer Science graduate student at the **University of Tehran**, studying Algorithms & Computation, with a BSc in Computer Engineering from **K. N. Toosi University of Technology**.

My interests center on machine learning, mathematical optimization, and the foundations of deep learning. I'm currently exploring overparameterization, generalization, and efficient computation in neural networks.

Alongside research, I work as a full-stack software engineer, designing and developing real-world software systems.
